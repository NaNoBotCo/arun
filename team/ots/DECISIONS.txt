15. Protection map. The E02 wetland run at all 10,201 positions the page can set (every 0.01 of the valley each way), default storm and wetland size, computed ahead by tools/protection-map.mjs and packed in src/wonder/protection-map.data.js. When the wetland moves, this device reruns that spot and the line under the map says whether the two agree, to the second. Kernel tests recompute five spots and require exact equality. With any other storm or wetland size the page says the map does not apply.

16. Two map layers: hours the hotel stays dry, and how much lower the river's peak is downstream. The second is a benefit to everyone below the valley and is shown in percent, not priced.

17. Why a data center, in a disclosure under the map: the map's measured CPU time, this device's measured time for one spot, and arithmetic for a 20 × 20 km river at 5 m cells, every step shown and labelled as arithmetic, not a server measurement.
